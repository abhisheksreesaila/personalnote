# Jam 2026-10-10: the captain's ideas (raw)

Spoken before a brainstorm session, back from vacation. Brainstorm only; nothing here is approved to build.

## Vision in the captain's words (lightly cleaned)

- It has to be **the best writing experience** for a person who wants to write. Markdown is the best today; start there.
- The moment people want to **draw and interact**, they can, especially **technical diagrams**.
- Maybe export to **slides** or a **HyperFrames** export. Not sure it fits the model; just an idea.
- Type and write, but **tear open any image and drop it in**; it can become a **whiteboarding session** for people.
- Mind maps, of course. A **plugin architecture**, of course, so people can plug things in.
- **Highly voice-activated.** Switch it on and it runs a **meeting**: captures audio, transcribes in real time, produces notes. Switch on, listen, switch off, transcribe and summarize, "boom", it gives you the information.
- **Mind maps with an agent thinking alongside you.** Mind maps are underused and beautiful because they are not linear. Today you are forced to think alone. Picture this: I add three ideas, and **ghost lines** appear: "here are five more you could consider". I pick one. I say "make it visual" and it adds emojis and hand-drawn diagrams. I say "expand this idea" and it **knows exactly which branch** I mean. A jamming session with an agent.
- **Agents are first-class citizens.**
- Summary: the best writing experience, the best audio-capture experience, and **Figma-like**: drop things in, connect them, and everything stays straightforward.
