01 · The difference

Not a notebook with an AI button.

Most tools bolt a chat panel onto a document and call it "AI-powered." Personal Note is built differently — the workspace itself speaks a protocol agents understand. No chat window. No prompt engineering. Just your notes, available to the tools you choose.

01 / SURFACE

One surface for prose and diagrams

No switching between a doc, a whiteboard, and a diagram tool. Write, place nodes, and draw freehand — all on a single canvas that expands with your thinking.

02 / PROTOCOL

Agents are native, not bolted on

The workspace exposes typed resources — notes, people, calendar drafts — through a semantic protocol. Any agent can query, propose, and enrich. No scraping. No fragile prompts.

03 / AMBIENT

Intelligence is ambient, not interruptive

Context cards appear when you pause and vanish when you resume. No permanent chat panel. No unsolicited suggestions. The notebook stays a notebook.
