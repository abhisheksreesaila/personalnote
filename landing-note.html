A page that grows.
And gives it back.

Like the best diagram tools, but for your thinking. There is always room, and never a blank infinity staring back at you.

01

Start on a page

Type, sketch, drop an image. It feels like paper because it is shaped like paper.

02

Drag past the edge

A new page slides in beside it, right where you need it. Keep going, it never runs out.

03

Clear it, it folds back

Delete what you don't need and the empty pages quietly tuck themselves away.

For your agents

Your agents can
read it too.

Claude Code, or any agent with a terminal, can search, read and append to your notes through one small command-line tool. Everything stays on your computer.

$ bin/personal-note search "passport" --text
#42 Passport renewal [Resources] - Renew by March
$ bin/personal-note notes read 42 --text
Renew by March. Photos at the pharmacy...
